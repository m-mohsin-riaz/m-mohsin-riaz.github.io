My name is Mohsin Riaz, and I am from Lahore. My journey in Computer Engineering began with unexpected challenges. During my 12th-grade exams, I had to undergo an appendix operation, which badly affected both my health and studies. Because of this, I lost one full academic year. It was a difficult time, as I had to focus on recovery while also dealing with the stress of falling behind. However, I decided not to give up. I worked hard to clear my supply exams and slowly returned to my studies. Preparing for the ECAT exam after a long gap was not easy, and I knew my preparation was not strong, but I stayed determined and gave my best. With hard work and consistency, I managed to pass the ECAT exam.

Alhamdulillah, I got admission to UET Faisalabad Campus, which was a big achievement for me. I completed all the admission formalities at the main campus and officially started my university journey. Moving from Lahore to Faisalabad and living in a hostel was a completely new experience. Although it was difficult in the beginning, it helped me become more independent and responsible.

This phase of my life taught me that setbacks are not the end. With patience and determination, it is always possible to move forward and achieve your goals.
