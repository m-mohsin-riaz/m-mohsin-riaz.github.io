🖥️ **A New World of Practical Learning**

One of the most exciting parts of starting university was attending my first computer lab. As a first-semester Computer Engineering student, I was looking forward to learning practical skills, but at the same time, I was also very nervous. I had heard that university labs were different from school computer classes, and I was not sure what to expect.

On the day of my first lab, I arrived a little early and entered the computer laboratory. The lab was well organized and equipped with modern computers, comfortable chairs, projectors, and high-speed internet. Seeing so many computers in one place made me excited because I knew that this was where I would learn many important technical skills during my university journey.

😰 **My First Day — Nervous and Confused**

To be honest, I was extremely nervous during my first lab. I did not know much about programming, and I had no idea what to do. When the teacher started talking about Python programming, many thoughts came into my mind. I wondered whether I would be able to understand the concepts or not. At that moment, I felt completely blank and confused. Everything seemed new and difficult.

Like many new students, I faced several difficulties during my first lab. Understanding programming concepts was not easy. Sometimes I could not understand the syntax of a program. Other times I would make small mistakes while typing code, which caused errors. Even finding and fixing those errors seemed difficult. There were moments when I felt frustrated because my program did not run correctly while others were completing their tasks.

---

🐍 **Learning Python — Step by Step**

However, Dr. Bilal never allowed us to lose confidence. He patiently guided every student and answered all our questions. Whenever someone faced a problem, he would explain the solution clearly. His teaching style was simple and easy to understand. Instead of making students feel afraid of programming, he encouraged us to keep practicing and learning from our mistakes.

During the first few labs, we started learning the fundamentals of programming using Python:

- 📦 **Variables and Data Types**
- ⌨️ **Input and Output Statements**
- ➕ **Operators and Expressions**
- 🧠 **Basic Programming Logic**

At first, these concepts looked difficult, but with practice and the teacher's guidance, they gradually became easier to understand.

---

📋 **Lab Rules We Followed**

The lab also had some important rules that every student was expected to follow:

- ⏰ Arrive on time
- 🤫 Maintain discipline and avoid disturbing others
- 💾 Save work properly after every session
- 🖥️ Use computers carefully
- 🧹 Keep the laboratory clean

Following these rules helped create a productive learning environment for everyone.

---

💪 **Growing Confidence**

As time passed, my confidence started to improve. The nervousness that I felt on the first day slowly disappeared. I began understanding programming concepts more clearly and became comfortable writing simple Python programs. Every new lab session taught me something useful. With regular practice and continuous support from Dr. Bilal, I started enjoying programming rather than fearing it.

One important lesson I learned was that **programming requires patience and practice**. Making mistakes is a normal part of learning. Every error teaches something new and helps improve problem-solving skills. I realized that even experienced programmers make mistakes, but they continue learning and improving.

---

🎯 **What I Took Away**

Looking back, my first computer lab experience was both challenging and rewarding. Although I was nervous and confused in the beginning, the support of Dr. Bilal and regular practice helped me overcome my difficulties. The lab introduced me to the world of programming and gave me confidence to continue learning new technologies.

I will always remember my first lab because it marked the beginning of my programming journey and inspired me to learn more about **Computer Engineering**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and **Machine Learning**.
