## From Comfort Zone to Hostel Life

---
My first night in the hostel was a completely new and emotional experience. Everything felt unfamiliar, and I did not know anyone around me. All the students were new, and no one knew each other. I quietly lay on my bed, trying to adjust to this new environment. It was the first time in my life that I was away from home, and I missed my family a lot. Even though it was difficult, I somehow managed to pass the night.

### My First Step into a New World

The next morning was my first day at university. I woke up early, got ready, and went to my department with other students. When we arrived, we first looked for our classroom. After some time, we were guided by our lectures and schedule. We also had a short introduction session with our classmates. At the beginning, it felt awkward, but slowly we started to feel comfortable with each other. After attending our classes, a few of us who had just become friends decided to explore the university.
### A Day of Discovery, Fear, and Excitement

We went to the café, but some seniors there stopped us. They asked for our introductions and gave us a few small tasks. It was a new experience, but we managed it. Later, we explored the campus, visited different departments, the library, playgrounds, and the mosque. After a long and tiring day, we returned to the hostel and went to sleep, completing our first day at university.
