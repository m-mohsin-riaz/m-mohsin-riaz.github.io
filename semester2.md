<div class="info-section">
  <h2>🌟 About This Semester</h2>
  <p>As I entered my second semester, I felt much more confident than I did in the first semester. By this time, I had already understood how university life works, how courses are managed, and how to balance lectures, labs, assignments, and exams. The nervousness of being a new student was mostly gone, and I felt more comfortable participating in class activities and discussions.</p>
  <p>The second semester was not only about academics but also about becoming more independent and responsible. Living away from home taught me many practical lessons about life. Hostel life came with its own challenges. Sometimes managing expenses was difficult because students have limited budgets and need to spend money carefully. There were also days when the mess food was not very good, and many students missed homemade meals.</p>
  <p>Despite these challenges, hostel life taught me important life skills. I learned how to manage my daily routine, handle responsibilities, and adjust to different situations. Sharing a room, living with students from different backgrounds, and solving everyday problems helped me become more mature and practical.</p>
  <p>This semester showed me that university is not only a place to study engineering. It is also a place where students learn how to face real-life challenges, become independent, and prepare themselves for the future. Every difficulty, whether academic or personal, became an opportunity to learn and grow.</p>
  <p>Looking back, I can say that the second semester made me stronger, more confident, and better prepared for the journey ahead.</p>
